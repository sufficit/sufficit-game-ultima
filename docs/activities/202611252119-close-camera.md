# Close third-person camera

## Investigation

The perspective cycle included an elevated orbit, first person and a
front-facing orbit. The player requested a close rear view with mouse
look for navigating at close range.

## Changes

- The third view starts 2.15 m behind the avatar. The shared
  [C# pose and aim calculation](../../engine/Ultima.Terrain/PlayerCloseCamera.cs)
  preserves the elevated orbit when the player switches back.
- Mouse look aligns the avatar with the camera. WASD can move laterally
  and backward while the avatar continues to aim with the view.
- The [browser adapter](../../src/wwwroot/js/mouse-look.js) requests
  pointer lock when entering the view from an eligible gesture. Escape
  releases the pointer. When capture is unavailable, moving the mouse
  over the focused scene also controls the view. The adapter batches
  mouse deltas to one update per animation frame.
- The garden and office use their existing collision queries to retract
  the close camera. Both hide the avatar if the camera gets too close.

## Validation and delivery

- Terrain WebAssembly, Release publish and RulesSmoke passed.
  Camera streaming, office desktop and mobile traversal, and keyboard,
  touch and gamepad regression scenarios passed locally.
- Chromium with SwiftShader verified the three views and mouse capture
  in both places with the garden worker and main-thread renderer. A
  headed Chromium run under Xvfb also verified native relative mouse
  motion, stationary aim and Escape release in the garden.
- The camera-views scenario passed in both modes on published revision
  `c808951afcfd7b6c177ddcac981a83f83a7d44cb`.
